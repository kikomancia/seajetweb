Freight Forwarding

Global Reach. Local Expertise. End-to-End Support.

NVOCC

Fast, Efficient and Strategically Routed Shipping

Sea-Jet delivers dependable NVOCC solutions powered by our strong carrier contracts and “next sailing” strategy, ensuring minimized transit times and optimized routing.

  • Consolidation of small-package cargo for efficient distribution
  • Routing through strategic ports for faster unloading and processing
  • Direct forwarding to inland destinations or turnover to your customs broker
  • Full container loads (FCL) delivered to the nearest port per your instructions
  • Professional Guidance on Shipping Requirem

Our established partnerships and deep industry knowledge allow us to deliver consistent, high-quality service in today’s fast-moving global market.
